Dearest Sahana,

Language often fails at the precise intersection of wonder and restraint. How does one explain that merely knowing you are somewhere in this same world makes the day lighter?

I have never desired to claim your time or dictate your future. My only goal has been to stand as a calm harbor—one where you are seen exactly as you are, cherished without conditions, and remembered without end.
